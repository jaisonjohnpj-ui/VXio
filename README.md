# VXio

VXio is an operations platform being developed to connect the people, projects, equipment and operational workflows behind a modern live-production business.

## What is VXio?

VXio is designed around a simple idea:

> Connect the operational parts of a company into one clear, traceable system.

The long-term vision is to connect:

- Projects and events
- People and crew
- Equipment and assets
- Warehouse and stock
- Scheduling
- Rental operations
- Finance
- Maintenance
- Communication
- Operational reporting

The goal is to reduce disconnected workflows and give teams a clearer operational picture from planning through completion.

## Product Vision

VXio is being designed as a serious operations system rather than a collection of isolated tools.

The platform is intended to support operational workflows across the lifecycle of a project, including planning, preparation, execution, return, maintenance, costing and financial processes.

## Design Principles

VXio is built around:

- Clarity
- Speed
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Controlled access
- Data integrity
- Scalability
- Consistent user experience

The interface should help operators work quickly without adding unnecessary complexity.

## Current Development

VXio is currently under active development.

The project is being developed incrementally, with product architecture and user experience decisions established before production implementation.

Current development areas include the core application workspace, dashboards, operational navigation, projects, equipment, warehouse workflows, financial workflows, scheduling and system administration.

## Project Status

🚧 **In active development**

VXio is currently a development project and is not presented as a finished or generally available commercial product.
